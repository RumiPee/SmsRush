# SMS Rush – Telegram Mini App

**Create. Send. Engage.**

A production-ready Telegram Mini App for professional SMS marketing campaign management, built for Netlify with serverless functions and server-side Telegram authentication.

---

## 📋 Overview

SMS Rush helps businesses, marketers, and organizations create, manage, and track permission-based SMS marketing campaigns. The app includes:

- Campaign creation with consent confirmation
- Smart SMS message generator
- Template management
- Opt-in contact management
- Campaign scheduling (local)
- Campaign history and export
- Full Telegram Mini App integration with server-side authentication

---

## 🔒 Consent-Based Messaging

SMS Rush is designed exclusively for **permission-based messaging**. The app enforces consent confirmation before any campaign can be saved or scheduled. Users must confirm that all recipients have provided valid consent to receive messages.

**You are responsible for complying with all applicable messaging, privacy, and carrier requirements.**
